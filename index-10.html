Privacy Policy — Schedule Lab

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Schedule Lab ("the App") is a personal time-management and daily scheduling app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

1. Data We Collect

The App asks for the following information directly from you when you use it:

  • Name and Gmail address — used only as a local identifier inside the App. This is not verified with Google and is not used for real Google sign-in.
  • Profile details — age, user type (student/employee/business/other), wake-up and sleep time, work/duty hours, and your main goal.
  • Profile photo — optional, if you choose to add one.
  • Schedule and task data — the tasks, goals, and timetable entries you create inside the App.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All of the data above is stored locally on your own device, using your browser/app's local storage. Schedule Lab does not operate a server or database, and we do not receive, see, or store a copy of your data on any Schedule Lab server.

Because your data lives only on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing app data will permanently delete it, unless you have used the in-app Export Backup feature to save a copy yourself.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Translate widget: If you use the in-app language translation dropdown, the page text is sent to Google's translation service to display the App in your chosen language. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The App does not use any advertising networks and does not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Notifications

If you enable Task Alerts, the App uses your browser/device's notification permission to remind you when a scheduled task begins. This works only while the App is open in your browser and does not send data anywhere — it is generated and shown entirely on your device.

5. Backup & Export

The Export/Import Backup feature creates a file containing your profile and schedule data, saved directly to your device's storage by you. This file is never uploaded anywhere by the App — it stays wherever you choose to save or share it.

6. Children's Privacy

Schedule Lab is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly target or collect data from children in a way that requires special handling, since no data leaves the user's own device.

7. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete any of your data at any time from within the App (Profile → Edit).
  • You can fully remove your data using the Logout (Reset App) option.
  • You can revoke notification permission anytime from your browser/device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

9. Contact Us
